							163 text ( b ) Advance written notice of hospital discharge rights.  For all Medicare Advantage enrollees, hospitals must deliver valid, written notice of an enrollee's rights as a hospital inpatient including discharge appeal rights. The hospital must use a standardized notice, as specified by CMS, in accordance with the following procedures:
						164 container
							165 text ( 1 ) Timing of notice.  The hospital must provide the notice at or near admission, but no later than 2 calendar days following the enrollee's admission to the hospital.

# Farmworker packet source check: bilingual stale insert

**Checked:** 2 November 2026. **Status:** Independently challenged, local research only. This is a supplementary two-publication narrow check, outside the 36-row agency ledger. It corroborates existing candidate NY-C04 and adds **zero** new issue clusters or approved findings.

The Department of Labor lists a [2026 English farmworker information packet](https://dol.ny.gov/system/files/documents/2026/01/aglp_2_workers_2026_english.pdf) and a [2026 Spanish packet](https://dol.ny.gov/system/files/documents/2026/03/aglp_2s_workers_2026_spanish.pdf). Both contain an early 2026 update saying overtime is due for farmwork beyond **52 hours** from 1 January 2026. Both also retain a later embedded worker-rights insert marked **P730 (1/24)** or **P730 S (1/24)**, saying overtime is due for work beyond **56 hours** from 1 January 2024. The 56-hour statement was accurate for the earlier threshold but, without a later-date qualification in that insert, is stale in a packet labeled 2026. A reader who consults only the later insert could miss overtime owed for hours 53–56 in a covered 2026 week. The earlier 52-hour update in each packet substantially mitigates that risk. No actual underpayment or reliance was established.

The current adopted [12 NYCRR § 190-2.4(c)](https://dol.ny.gov/farm-laborers-wage-board) sets 52 hours from 1 January 2026. DOL's updated standalone [English P730](https://dol.ny.gov/system/files/documents/2026/03/p730-h-2a-farm-workers-fs-3-26_0.pdf) and [Spanish P730 ES (3/26)](https://dol.ny.gov/your-rights-h-2a-worker-p730s-spanish) both correctly give 52 hours. The [Spanish 2026 labor-law booklet](https://dol.ny.gov/system/files/documents/2026/03/p737-2-26es-us.pdf) does too.

**Verdict:** This is an edition-assembly and current-guidance problem appearing in both languages, **not** an English-to-Spanish mistranslation. Count it as one additional manifestation of NY-C04, not a new finding. The large packet PDFs were inspected through official indexed text and the directly retrievable Spanish text; local stable binaries and visual page checks have not yet been saved. Before any public use, capture both packet binaries, inspect the exact page images, and confirm the official catalog still links them.

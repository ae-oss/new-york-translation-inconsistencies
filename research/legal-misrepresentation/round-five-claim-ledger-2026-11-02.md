**Method for the form rows:** PDF text extraction found the Question 12 boundary and seven checkbox glyphs in each of the 13 saved translated applications. The current English EA04/23 application has eight named denial choices, including NSA. The absence of an NSA option was semantically checked in Spanish, Traditional-script Chinese, French, Russian, and Hindi; English and Spanish Question 12 were also checked visually in rendered page images. Structural checks do not identify which translated label, if any, differs in the other eight forms. The Hindi form mentions NSA elsewhere, in Question 13; the confirmed omission is the Question 12 choice.

**Separate source checks:** The DOH [Complaints and Appeals index](https://healthweb-back.health.ny.gov/health_care/managed_care/complaints/) directly links row 10, but was checked only for link provenance. A second DOH navigation page was also a link check. These are not counted above. An older DOH [model handbook explicitly revised for 2010](https://healthweb-back.health.ny.gov/health_care/managed_care/medicaid_managed_model_member_handbook.htm) has 45-day language; it is excluded from current-rule findings because it is visibly obsolete.

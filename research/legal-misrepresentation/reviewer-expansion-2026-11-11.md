# Independent review of the 11 November 2026 expansion

This ledger records my separate challenge of the two investigator lanes. It does not certify whole documents or every language. The worker/housing lane examined WCB and HCR publications; the education/elections lane examined NYSED and BOE publications. The prior-work check used the candidate register, round-two through round-five checkpoints, and the evidence file inventory. WCB C-3 had previously been touched for a different clause, and HCR lease/Fact Sheets 4, 9, 16, 18, 26 and 40 had earlier checks; neither was counted as a new *document* solely because a new clause was found.

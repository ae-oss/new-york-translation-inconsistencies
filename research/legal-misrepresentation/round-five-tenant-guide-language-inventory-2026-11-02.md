## Narrow English–Haitian Creole edition check

An independent reviewer compared the archived [2020 English PDF](https://ag.ny.gov/sites/default/files/publications/2020-tenantrights-final_1.pdf) with the linked [Haitian Creole PDF](https://ag.ny.gov/sites/default/files/publications/2020-tenantrights-creole.pdf). Both have 71 PDF pages, the same eleven table-of-contents sections in the same order, and the same printed section starts: housing 6, leases 10, rent 16, succession/termination 26, habitability 38, safety 44, utilities 50, personal protections 52, manufactured homes 58, finding an apartment 62, and resources 64. Selected matching markers include the nonregulated rent-increase threshold on PDF pages 12 and 18, pre–14 June 2019 deregulation on page 18, and individual-apartment-improvement cap examples on page 20. Both versions say **more than 5% / plis pase 5%**, so that sampled passage does **not** show a translation difference. This is a **probable edition-aligned candidate pair**, based on sampled structure and passages. Full page-by-page identity and translation provenance remain unverified; no completed pair review is counted. The historical English PDF is not assumed to represent current law.

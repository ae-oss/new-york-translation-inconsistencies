## Other observations and exclusions

- **Limited historical-report error:** DHR's [FY2023–24 annual report](https://dhr.ny.gov/system/files/documents/2026/01/nys-dhr-sfy-2024-annual-report.pdf) gives 15 February 2024 in its timeline and 15 February 2025 in the next narrative for the same three-year complaint-rule transition. [Chapter 656 of 2023](https://www.nysenate.gov/legislation/bills/2023/S3255) and current DHR operational material support 2024. The independent reviewer did not promote the isolated annual-report sentence to a major current-guidance finding.
- **Unresolved, not counted as a candidate:** The Attorney General tenant guide's nonrenewal summary may need a more nuanced [Good Cause](https://ag.ny.gov/publications/new-york-state-good-cause-eviction-law) analysis of coverage and court procedure. The prior checkpoint withheld a verdict.
- **Excluded historic template:** The DOH model Medicaid handbook marked “Revised for 2010” says 45 days for external appeals. That is not evidence of a current template or a separate live-rule finding.

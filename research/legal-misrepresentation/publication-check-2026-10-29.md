For the hearing issue, Labor Law [§ 620(1)(a)](https://www.nysenate.gov/legislation/laws/LAB/620) states the statutory request window after mailing or personal delivery. The Board's procedural rule specifies postmark and receipt presumptions. The DOL's [Hearing Process FAQ](https://dol.ny.gov/hearing-process-frequently-asked-questions) gives more complete filing-method guidance. A current agency-posted rule and the Board's May 2026 application support the rule's present operation; targeted searches found no conflicting later amendment, but were not an exhaustive audit of State Register issues or the official hardcopy supplement. The case page states that limit. This project does not decide whether the rule exceeds statutory authority.

Both case pages use hypothetical potential consequences. No actual lost benefit, missed hearing, correction of the cited DOL pages, or individual reliance has been established. The signed bill's final Part M, rather than earlier amendments on the same page, is the sunset source. The COVID case does not assert that ordinary sick leave or all other protections expired.
